# Your first research article

Welcome to your COSMOS research library. **This is a starter template, not a finished science article.** Replace this text with your own research when you're ready.

## The question

What question are you trying to answer? Start with one clear question rather than trying to explain an entire subject at once.

## What I found

Write your explanation in your own words. Split big ideas into short sections and define any scientific terms that a new reader might not know.

You can use **bold text**, *italics*, and `inline code` in your notes.

## What I'm still wondering

- What do I understand better now?
- What is still uncertain or debated?
- What question should I investigate next?

## Sources and further reading

List the sources you actually used. For example:

- [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/) — add the exact page or article you used.
- Add the author, title, publisher, and link for other sources.

Always check that a source supports the claim you're making. Separate established evidence from hypotheses and your own questions.
